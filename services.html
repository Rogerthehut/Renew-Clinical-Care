Our proposed services

Focused postnatal care at home.

The initial service is being designed for mothers and babies who are clinically stable and need planned, short-term nursing support following birth or hospital discharge.

PROPOSED SCOPE

What support may include.

Every future care package would require an individual assessment, a suitable clinical plan and clear agreement about professional responsibility.

♡

Recovery observations

Planned observations of the mother’s recovery, symptoms and general wellbeing, in line with an agreed clinical plan.

＋

Baby wellbeing

Planned observations of the baby’s wellbeing, in line with an agreed postnatal care plan.

◷

Feeding support

Practical support with feeding, informed by evidence, where assessed and included in the care plan.

⌂

Family guidance

Practical education, reassurance and clear information about what to expect during the early postnatal period.

↗

Escalation

Defined steps for concerns, including contact with the responsible clinician or urgent services.

◎

Clinical liaison

Proportionate updates and handover with the family’s maternity or healthcare team, with appropriate consent.

Who the initial service may suit.

  • Mothers and babies during the early postnatal period
  • Families in Bromley and an agreed local catchment
  • Needs that can be safely supported at home for a limited period
  • Care with a clear clinical plan and escalation route
  • Privately funded or commissioned arrangements, once available

Not an emergency service

Renew Clinical Care will not replace emergency, acute hospital or primary care services. Mothers or babies who are unwell, clinically unstable or need continuous acute intervention would not be suitable for this service.

In an emergency, call 999. For urgent advice, use NHS 111 or contact the responsible maternity or clinical team.
