Frequently asked questions

Clear answers at an early stage.

The service is still being developed. These answers explain the intended model without promising care before registration and readiness are confirmed.

Is Renew Clinical Care currently providing nursing care?

No. Renew Clinical Care is pre-launch and preparing for CQC registration. Regulated care will not be provided, accepted or scheduled until the appropriate registration and operating safeguards are in place.

What service is being developed?

A short-term, nurse-led service for children and their families at home following hospital discharge, surgery, treatment or illness, initially focused on Bromley and an agreed local area.

Who may be eligible?

The proposed service is for children who are clinically stable and whose needs can be safely met through planned home visits. Final eligibility criteria will be governed by clinical assessment, competencies, geography and the relevant discharge plan.

Can a parent contact Renew directly?

Yes, parents and carers can make a preliminary enquiry or register an interest. Once operational, suitability may also require information and liaison from the responsible hospital or clinical team.

Can a hospital or clinical professional make a referral now?

Not a patient referral. Healthcare professionals can discuss future pathways or anticipated demand. They should not send information that identifies a patient or any clinical information through the website.

How quickly after discharge would support begin?

This will depend on assessment, availability, safe handover and a written plan. No commitment about a start date can be made during the pre-launch period.

Will the service replace NHS or hospital care?

No. The proposed service is supplementary. Medical responsibility, emergency care and specialist follow-up remain with the appropriate healthcare professionals.

What happens if my child becomes unwell?

The future care plan would include clear escalation arrangements. This website is not an emergency or clinical advice service. Call 999 for a medical emergency, or NHS 111 or the responsible clinical team for urgent advice as appropriate.

How will care be funded?

Potential privately funded and commissioned arrangements are being explored. Pricing, payment terms and insurer or hospital pathways have not yet been finalised.

Is my information safe?

The pre-launch form asks only for basic contact information. Please do not include diagnoses, NHS numbers, medical records or detailed information about your child. Formal clinical information would use an approved secure process once the service is operational.

Still have a question?

Send a preliminary enquiry without clinical details.
