The proposed service

Focused support when it matters most.

The initial service is intended for mothers and babies who would benefit from planned, short-term postnatal nursing support following birth or hospital discharge.

  • Postnatal observations and recovery support for mother
  • Newborn wellbeing observations within an agreed plan
  • Feeding support, education and practical reassurance
  • Clear family guidance and escalation support
  • Liaison with maternity, neonatal or primary care teams where appropriate
