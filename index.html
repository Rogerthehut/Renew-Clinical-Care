The proposed service

Focused support when it matters most.

The initial service is intended for children and their families who would benefit from planned, short-term nursing support following hospital discharge, surgery, treatment or illness.

  • Planned observations of the child's recovery and wellbeing
  • Wound care, medication support and symptom monitoring within an agreed plan
  • Practical education and reassurance for the whole family
  • Clear family guidance and escalation support
  • Liaison with hospital, paediatric or primary care teams where appropriate
