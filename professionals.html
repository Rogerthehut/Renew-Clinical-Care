For healthcare professionals

Extend paediatric care into the home.

Renew Clinical Care is developing a nurse-led model for short-term nursing support following hospital discharge, surgery, treatment or illness, with clear handover, defined scope and proportionate clinical communication.

PAEDIATRIC WARDS • HOSPITALS • CLINICAL TEAMS

A credible continuity partner.

The intention is to complement, rather than replace, the responsible hospital, medical and community teams.

Potential partnerships may include planned pathways with private hospitals, paediatric consultants and other children’s healthcare services for suitable families in Bromley and the agreed local catchment.

Designed around safe handover.

  • Defined eligibility and exclusions
  • Verified clinical information
  • Named clinical responsibility
  • Documented escalation pathway
  • Outcome and completion communication
Proposed referral pathway

From early notification to completion.

The final pathway will be confirmed through governance work and CQC registration.

01 • NOTIFY

Initial contact

Share the timing and broad support requirement. Do not provide information that identifies a patient through this website.

02 • SCREEN

Suitability review

Confirm geography, acuity, competencies, resources and proposed duration.

03 • HANDOVER

Secure transfer

Receive discharge summary, plan, consent and professional contacts securely.

04 • CLOSE

Completion

Provide agreed updates and a clear handover when the service ends.

Information likely to be required.

Clinical

Diagnosis, treatment or surgery details, relevant history, the child’s current condition, expected recovery and medication plans.

Responsibility

Named consultant or team, arrangements outside normal hours, escalation thresholds and review date.

Practical

Home location, family consent, equipment, visit requirements, funding and proposed start date.

Information governance: the website form is for preliminary organisational enquiries only. Do not send patient names, NHS numbers, clinical records or other special category data.
