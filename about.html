About Renew

Paediatric nursing, thoughtfully brought home.

Renew Clinical Care is a nurse-led service being developed in Bromley to bridge the gap between hospital discharge and recovery at home. Its purpose is to support children and their families through the transition from hospital to home, following surgery, treatment or illness.

FOUNDER PROFILE • DETAILS TO BE CONFIRMED

Led by an experienced Children’s Nurse.

The service is being founded by a registered Children’s Nurse with experience supporting babies, children and families in acute and community care.

The final website will include the founder’s name, professional qualifications, NMC registration details, clinical background and areas of specialist experience following verification and approval.

Why this matters: families should know who is accountable for the care provided to them and their child. Referring professionals should also be able to verify clinical leadership.

Our purpose

To give children and their families a clinically sound, reassuring bridge between hospital discharge and confident family life at home.

  • Clinically responsible
  • Warm and centred on the family
  • Clear about scope and limits
  • Collaborative with hospital teams
What will guide us

Quiet confidence, visible standards.

01

Safety first

Care planning, competency, safeguarding, escalation and documentation will underpin every service decision.

02

One family, one plan

Support will respond to the needs of the child, their clinical plans and the family’s circumstances.

03

Coordinated care

Clear handover and communication with the responsible clinical team will be central to the model.

Building the service properly.

Renew Clinical Care is currently in development. This includes preparing governance arrangements, policies, insurance, workforce controls and the application for CQC registration.

  • No regulated activity will begin before the required registration is granted
  • Final clinical scope and eligibility criteria remain subject to governance review
  • Professional credentials and company information will be verified before public launch
